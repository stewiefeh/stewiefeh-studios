The idea

Tangent is a fictional twelve-person studio in New York for “restless brands.” The one-line pitch on the site is We design the unreasonable, so the site itself had to live up to it. I wanted the first second on the page to feel like touching something, not reading something: the background is a shader that answers your cursor, and the studio’s work sits in a gallery you physically drag sideways instead of scroll past.

The build

The whole site is one hand-written HTML file with no libraries. A GLSL fragment shader paints the background and bends it toward the cursor in real time. The selected-work gallery is scroll-pinned and draggable, so visitors move through two years of projects at their own pace. Page changes arrive as full-bleed wipes rather than jumps, and the header clock ticks live in New York time, a small detail that makes a fiction feel staffed.
