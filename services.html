Questions people ask

How long until my site is live?

A landing page is about a week from the go-ahead. A full site usually takes two to three, and the main variable is how fast I get your photos and the facts about your business.

What happens if the sample isn't right?

Tell me what missed and I'll take another pass. If it still isn't right, you walk away owing nothing. That's the whole point of building the sample first.

Do you write the words, or do I?

Both. On a landing page you send what you have and I lay it out so it reads well. On a full site I write the copy or clean up yours, whichever you prefer.

I already have a website. Does that change anything?

Not the price. If a rebuild makes sense I move whatever is worth keeping over and start fresh where it isn't. If your current site is doing its job, I'll tell you that too.

Found your plan?

Tell me the business name and what you'd want the site to do, and I'll come back with a plan and a price.
