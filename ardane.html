atelier concept · bronze & smoked oak

Ardane slow is a method,
not a mood.

A concept site for a two-person atelier in Portland, Maine: bronze and smoked oak, twelve commissions a year, never more. The design had to feel like the work itself — heavy, patient, exact.

The idea

Ardane makes objects slowly and says so everywhere: the bench takes one new commission a month, the ledger is honest about the wait, and every piece leaves the workshop with a name and its maker’s initials struck into the underside. Most craft sites whisper and then behave like every other template. I wanted this one to whisper and move — proof that quiet and animated are not opposites.

The build

The scroll is virtual and smoothed, so the page glides instead of stepping. Headlines split into characters and settle in one by one, and sections reveal through clip-path wipes that read like a casting being pulled from its mold. The material ledger — bronze, smoked oak, patina, walnut — sits in a plain honest table, because a workshop that values honesty should not hide its prices behind a form.
