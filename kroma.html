The idea

Kroma’s pitch is that it refuses to whisper: no templates, no recycled layouts, no beige. So the site had to be the loudest of the three builds while staying sharp instead of messy. Color does the structural work — sections are held apart by hue, not boxes — and the copy keeps a dry, confident voice under all of it.

The build

The intro splits the studio’s name into letters that arrive one at a time, and a rolling marquee carries the service words across the page without stealing focus. The work is presented as a plain list with each project’s own identity, then a facts grid gives the studio’s numbers straight (founded 2024, a handful of projects a year, far too many color palettes hoarded). Everything animates at 60fps on a phone, not just on a good laptop.
